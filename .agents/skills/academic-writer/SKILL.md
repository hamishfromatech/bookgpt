# Academic Writer Skill

## Overview

This skill provides specialized guidance for academic and scholarly writing, including:
- Formal academic tone and terminology
- Literature review integration and synthesis
- Methodological description and justification
- Citation formatting (APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, etc.)
- Peer-review style structure and expectations
- Argumentation based on evidence and research

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Writing textbooks or educational monographs
- Producing academic research papers or articles
- Creating scholarly books or reference works
- Writing dissertations or theses
- Developing content for academic journals or conferences

## Academic Writing Best Practices

### 1. Structure and Format
- **Introduction:** State research question, thesis, and paper structure
- **Literature Review:** Synthesize existing research, identify gaps
- **Methodology:** Describe methods, justification, limitations
- **Results/Findings:** Present data objectively
- **Discussion:** Interpret findings, relate to literature
- **Conclusion:** Summarize, suggest future research

### 2. Tone and Style
- Formal, objective language
- Avoid first-person pronouns (unless discipline-specific)
- Use precise terminology and define specialized terms
- Maintain neutral, unbiased tone

### 3. Citation and Referencing
- Follow specific citation style consistently (APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, Harvard)
- Cite all claims that are not common knowledge
- Include in-text citations and reference list/bibliography
- Verify all source information for accuracy

### 4. Argumentation and Evidence
- Base arguments on empirical evidence or logical reasoning
- Acknowledge counterarguments and limitations
- Use data, statistics, or quotes to support claims
- Avoid speculation without evidence

## Discipline-Specific Considerations

| Discipline | Key Elements |
|------------|--------------|
| Sciences/STEM | Methodological rigor, data presentation, reproducibility |
| Humanities | Interpretive analysis, theoretical frameworks, textual evidence |
| Social Sciences | Research design, participant ethics, statistical analysis |
| Medicine/Health | Clinical evidence, ethical considerations, patient outcomes |
