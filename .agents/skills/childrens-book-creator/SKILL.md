# Children's Book Creator Skill

## Overview

This skill provides specialized guidance for children's book writing, including:
- Age-appropriate vocabulary and sentence structure
- Educational themes and moral lessons
- Engaging storytelling with repetition and rhythm
- Illustration prompts and visual elements
- Safety and appropriateness of content
- Interactive or educational components

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Writing picture books for ages 3-8
- Creating early reader books (ages 5-8)
- Developing chapter books for middle grade (ages 8-12)
- Writing educational or instructional children's content
- Creating stories with moral lessons or life skills

## Children's Book Writing Best Practices

### 1. Age Appropriateness
| Age Range | Word Count | Reading Level | Themes |
|-----------|------------|---------------|--------|
| 0-3 (Board Books) | 50-200 words | Simplest terms | Basic concepts, sounds, routines |
| 3-8 (Picture Books) | 500-1000 words | Early reader | Friendship, family, emotions, discovery |
| 8-12 (Chapter Books) | 10,000-20,000 words | Middle grade | Adventure, school, growing up, identity |

### 2. Language and Style
- Use simple, clear vocabulary
- Short sentences for younger readers
- Repetition and rhyme for engagement
- Active voice rather than passive
- Sensory details that children can relate to

### 3. Story Structure for Children
- **Introduction:** Introduce character and setting simply
- **Problem/Conflict:** Something happens that needs solving
- **Resolution:** Character finds solution or learns lesson
- **Conclusion:** Happy or satisfying ending appropriate for age

### 4. Educational Themes
- Friendship and sharing
- Courage and trying new things
- Family and community
- Nature and discovery
- Emotional intelligence and empathy

## Illustration Integration

Children's books are highly visual. When writing:
- Leave space for illustrations (typically 12-32 pages for picture books)
- Describe scenes visually but don't over-write
- Consider page turns for dramatic effect
- Provide illustration notes if self-publishing or working with an artist

## Tool Integration with BookGPT

When using this skill with BookGPT:
1. Ensure the agent uses age-appropriate language in writing phase
2. Request illustration prompts for specific scenes via Agent Chat
3. Use running summary to maintain character consistency across pages
4. Review chapters for appropriate pacing and page turns

## Example Prompts for Children's Books

- "Rewrite this chapter using simpler vocabulary for ages 5-7"
- "Add a rhyme or repetition pattern to make it more engaging"
- "Generate illustration prompts for pages 3, 5, and 7"
- "Ensure the moral lesson is clear but not preachy"

## Validation Checklist for Children's Chapters

- [ ] Language appropriate for target age group
- [ ] Story has clear beginning, middle, and end
- [ ] Character is relatable and engaging
- [ ] Conflict is age-appropriate (not too scary or complex)
- [ ] Resolution teaches a positive lesson or provides satisfaction
- [ ] Pacing allows for illustration breaks
- [ ] Themes are appropriate and valuable for children
