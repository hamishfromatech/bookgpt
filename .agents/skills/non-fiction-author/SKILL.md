# Non-Fiction Author Skill

## Overview

This skill provides specialized guidance for non-fiction writing, including:
- Research methodology and citation integration
- Logical argument structure and flow
- Reader education and knowledge transfer
- Authoritative yet accessible tone
- Fact-checking and accuracy verification
- Practical application and actionable takeaways

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Writing instructional or educational books
- Developing business, self-help, or professional guides
- Creating memoirs or biographical works
- Writing technical or scientific explanations
- Producing content that requires factual accuracy and citations

## Non-Fiction Writing Best Practices

### 1. Structure and Organization
- **Introduction:** Establish relevance, state thesis, outline structure
- **Body Chapters:** Logical progression, each building on previous
- **Conclusion:** Summarize key points, provide call-to-action or final thoughts

### 2. Research and Citation
- Verify facts from multiple reliable sources
- Cite appropriately within text or footnotes
- Distinguish between established fact and opinion
- Include recent data where relevant

### 3. Tone and Style
- Authoritative but accessible
- Avoid jargon unless defined
- Use examples and case studies to illustrate points
- Maintain consistency in voice throughout

### 4. Reader Engagement
- Start chapters with a hook or relevant question
- Use subheadings to guide readers through complex topics
- Include summaries or key takeaways at chapter ends
- Provide practical exercises or reflection questions

## Genre-Specific Considerations

| Genre | Key Elements |
|-------|--------------|
| Business/Leadership | Case studies, actionable frameworks, leadership principles |
| Self-Help | Step-by-step guidance, relatable examples, transformation focus |
| Memoir/Biography | Personal narrative, historical context, emotional truth |
| Technical/Scientific | Accurate terminology, clear explanations, visual aids where possible |
| History/Culture | Contextual background, primary sources, objective analysis |

When using this skill with BookGPT:
1. Ensure thorough research notes are generated in Phase 2
2. Use `grep_search` to verify factual consistency across chapters

## Example Prompts for Non-Fiction Writing

- "Add a case study to illustrate the framework in chapter 4"
- "Verify the statistical claim on page 3 and provide source context"
- "Rewrite this section to be more accessible for beginners"
- "Add a summary of key takeaways at the end of chapter 6"

## Validation Checklist for Non-Fiction Chapters

- [ ] Clear thesis or main point stated early
- [ ] Logical progression of ideas
- [ ] Supporting evidence or examples provided
- [ ] Factual accuracy verified
- [ ] Tone appropriate for target audience
- [ ] Practical application or takeaways included
- [ ] Transitions between sections are smooth
