# Fiction Writer Skill

## Overview

This skill provides specialized guidance for fiction writing, including:
- Character development arcs and psychology
- Plot structure (setup, rising action, climax, resolution)
- Scene and chapter structuring
- Dialogue that reveals character and advances plot
- Pacing and tension management
- Genre conventions and reader expectations

## When to Use

Use this skill when:
- Writing a novel or short story
- Developing fictional characters and their arcs
- Structuring plot points and story beats
- Writing dialogue-heavy scenes
- Working within specific fiction genres (fantasy, sci-fi, mystery, romance, etc.)

## Fiction Writing Best Practices

### 1. Character Development
- Give characters clear motivations and goals
- Create internal and external conflicts
- Show character growth through actions, not just dialogue
- Use backstory selectively to inform present actions

### 2. Plot Structure
- **Setup (Chapters 1-25%):** Introduce protagonist, world, and inciting incident
- **Rising Action (25%-75%):** Build tension, introduce obstacles, develop relationships
- **Climax (75%-90%):** Peak conflict, protagonist faces main challenge
- **Resolution (90%-100%):** Consequences, character growth, new status quo

### 3. Scene Structure
Each scene should have:
- A goal for the protagonist
- Conflict or obstacle
- A resolution that changes the situation or character

### 4. Dialogue Quality
- Reveal character through speech patterns
- Use dialogue to advance plot or reveal information
- Avoid "talking heads" - include action beats and internal reactions
- Subtext is often more powerful than direct statement

## Genre-Specific Considerations

| Genre | Key Elements |
|-------|--------------|
| Fantasy | World-building, magic systems, mythic themes |
| Sci-Fi | Technical plausibility, future/space settings, philosophical questions |
| Mystery | Clues, red herrings, logical resolution, investigator protagonist |
| Romance | Emotional connection, relationship development, satisfying conclusion |
| Thriller | Pacing, tension, stakes, suspenseful scenes |

## Example Prompts for Fiction Writing

- "Expand chapter 3's opening scene with more sensory details and internal reaction"
- "Develop the antagonist's motivation to make their actions more understandable"
- "Add tension to the confrontation scene between [Character A] and [Character B]"
- "Show, don't tell, the protagonist's grief after the loss in chapter 5"

## Validation Checklist for Fiction Chapters

- [ ] Protagonist has a clear goal in the scene
- [ ] Conflict or obstacle is present
- [ ] Character growth or change occurs (even if subtle)
- [ ] Dialogue reveals character or advances plot
- [ ] Sensory details ground the scene in the setting
- [ ] Pacing matches the emotional intensity of the moment
